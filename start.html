Beta · how to test (one screen)

  1. Open the app on your phone and tap Try it on this phone. Your data stays on this phone; no account. Android: Chrome · iPhone: Safari
  2. Add it to your home screen, then start it from there. Android: ⋮ → Add to home screen · iPhone: Share ↑ → Add to Home Screen
  3. Answer the questions and add your plant (a few taps). Log whenever you look at the plant; photos help.
  4. Today → Check-in with your AI → Send, pick your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT…). Photos go along.
  5. Bring the answer back. Android: in the AI app, Share the answer → Flowerphile · iPhone: copy the answer, open Flowerphile, tap Paste your AI's answer You see every change first; tick what you want, then Apply.
  6. After a step (topping, a water change…): log it, then tap Ask your AI about this step. The answer shows under the log.
  7. Optional, Claude users: install the Flowerphile skill so Claude knows the format from the start. Download below → claude.ai → Settings → Capabilities → Skills → upload the ZIP
